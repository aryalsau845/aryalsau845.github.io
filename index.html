About

I am a PhD candidate in Civil Engineering at Michigan State University and an Engineer-in-Training (E.I.T.). My background bridges numerical hydrology, engineering analysis, and the study of river and reservoir systems.

At the Multi-scale FEW Lab, I apply hydrologic and hydrodynamic modeling, satellite observations, climate information, and geospatial analysis to examine water availability, flood hazards, and human influences on river systems. I am especially interested in translating strong modeling and analytical skills into practical water-resources and resilience work.
